# Pipeline — productions

Template. The interview replaces the placeholders; delete stages that don't
exist here and add the ones that do.

## 1. Pitch / quote
- **Enters:** an enquiry (referral, site form, repeat client, agency).
- **Leaves:** a job folder with `brief.md` started; a quote sent; the reasoning for the price in `decisions.md`.
- **Owner:** <who quotes>
- **Goes wrong:** quoting before the scope is clear; not writing down what was promised.
- **Assistant:** creates the job; drafts the treatment if asked.

## 2. Brief
- **Enters:** a yes from the client.
- **Leaves:** `brief.md` complete: what they want, why, audience, deliverables, dates, sign-off person.
- **Goes wrong:** the brief lives in an email thread and nobody reads it on the day.

## 3. Pre-production
- **Leaves:** shot list, call sheet, crew assigned, kit list, locations confirmed, releases.
- **Owner:** <producer>
- **Goes wrong:** parking, power, permissions, weather plan.
- **Assistant:** shot list, call sheet, crew assignments from profiles.

## 4. Shoot day(s)
- **Leaves:** footage, a note of what wasn't got and why.
- **Goes wrong:** anything not in the call sheet.
- **Assistant:** nothing on the day. Capture afterwards: "what didn't we get?"

## 5. Ingest and triage
- **Leaves:** footage backed up in two places; usable takes flagged.
- **Boundary:** if an editing agent exists, hand over `brief.md` and the shot list here. Nothing else.

## 6. Edit
- **Leaves:** a first cut for review; `status.md` says who's editing and when it's due.
- **Boundary:** the assistant never touches the timeline.

## 7. Client review rounds
- **Leaves:** per round, a client-facing recap in `outputs/` and the client's asks in `decisions.md`.
- **Goes wrong:** round 3 feedback contradicting round 1; nobody has the record.

## 8. Delivery
- **Leaves:** delivery note in `outputs/`: what went out, format, where, when, to whom.

## 9. Archive and invoice
- **Leaves:** archive checklist done; invoice raised and dated in `status.md`.
- **Assistant:** end-of-job review runs here.
